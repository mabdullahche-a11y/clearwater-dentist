Dental implants

An implant is a fixture placed in the jaw that can support a replacement tooth.

Who it’s for

For people with missing teeth when their health, gums and bone are suitable.

Typical duration
Usually several months across stages
Price
Price on request

What to expect

  1. Review your health and assess the site
  2. Place the implant if suitable and allow healing
  3. Fit the replacement tooth and plan ongoing care

General information only. Your dentist will advise on what's right for you after an examination.

Common questions

Is bone grafting always needed?

It depends on the bone available at the proposed site.
