Questions before your visit

How do I request an appointment?

Send a WhatsApp message or use the booking form. The clinic confirms the date and time by reply.

Can I ask for an estimate first?

Yes. Ask what the consultation costs and when you can expect a written treatment estimate.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a medication and allergy list and any recent dental records or images you have.

Dental emergency?

Seek urgent dental care for facial swelling or dental trauma. Get emergency medical care for uncontrolled bleeding, serious facial injury or swelling that affects breathing.
