Privacy

How the website handles appointment information and external services.

What happens to your information

Appointment requests

The form prepares a message in your browser. When you continue, WhatsApp receives the prefilled text and lets you decide whether to send it. Please leave medical records and identity numbers out of the form.

External services

WhatsApp, map embeds and any Instagram videos are operated by their respective providers. They may process information such as your IP address when loaded or opened.

Browser storage

The website can remember that you dismissed the demo banner for the current session.
